The Book

The Cafe — The Story of The Ground

Some doors are better left closed.

James Stout is a husband, a father — an ordinary man living an ordinary life in an ordinary town. The only thing remarkable about his weekends is The Cafe, a local bar where good music, cold beer and old friends make the world feel simple.

Until the night it doesn’t.

Pulled through a portal into a world of dark magic and an ancient evil, James discovers that nothing about his life has ever been ordinary.

Guided by a wizard carrying six hundred years of secrets and a hunter with nothing left to lose, James must reclaim who he truly is before an evil that knows him better than he knows himself finishes what it started.

But James Stout has never been very good at doing what he’s told.

The Cafe — The Story of The Ground is an epic fantasy adventure about identity, sacrifice and the price of power. And what it truly means to be home.

About the Author

Tiago Moreira

I have always found pleasure in exploring other worlds through the countless words written in the books I have read throughout my life. Creativity and imagination have always been, and always will be, one of the most valuable qualities of human beings — and that is what I try to show my son and daughter every day through books, music, painting and science.

We can all be better by creating something and inspiring someone to try something new and different.

This is my first book, but we will meet again and explore other worlds, other journeys.

Until then, I hope you enjoy the world of The Ground.
